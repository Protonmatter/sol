disk · network

Cells that boil in place

Two Worley layers give the supergranular network and bright points; ridged noise adds fine filaments. Gradients rotate over time (flow noise) and cell sites orbit their seeds, so the surface evolves without sliding across the sphere.

disk · dark regions

Torn, textured holes

A domain-warped threshold, roughened at two finer scales, cut to about 40% brightness. The network stays visible inside, which is what separates the references from a cow-print mask.

disk · active regions

Dipole fan arcs

Contours of the angle two poles subtend give the butterfly pattern: arcs joining the poles and fans spraying outward. Each arc gets its own brightness and flicker, over a hot white core pair and a diffuse plage.

off-limb

Rim, fur, loops and prominences

An emissive rim, radial streaks stretched along height, concentric loop arcs where an active region sits near the limb, and turbulent prominence arches drawn in their own redder channel.

composition

One palette after bloom

The scene writes scalar intensity to a float target. Bloom blurs that intensity, then a single palette maps it to colour, so glow and cores share the ramp and highlights roll off to white instead of clipping.

level of detail

Detail follows pixel size

Fine terms fade in with the disk's size in pixels. At Overview size the Sun is a glowing disk with a rim; detail arrives as it grows, so nothing shimmers in the Solar System view.
